An Amiga 500 arcade puzzle game, inspired by the 1986 MSX game Snake It.

THE GAME
  A puzzle snake: the snake only moves when you do - one step for every
  push of the stick (hold it to keep going). Think before you move!

  Eat every clover. Each clover makes the snake longer - and makes a
  white ball pop up somewhere you can reach. The balls must be eaten too,
  but they don't make you grow. A round is clear when every clover and
  every ball is gone.

  SPORES: the little red dots. Every time you eat something (a clover or
  a ball), a poison mushroom grows on the next spot - the blinking one.
  So the order in which you eat changes the field! Is the spot covered
  by your tail, a crate or the heart when it is its turn? Then nothing
  grows there and that spot is gone for good. Use your body to keep the
  narrow passages open.

  CRATES: walk into a crate to push it one step (if there is room behind
  it). Open blocked passages, build a safe path for your long tail, or
  cover a spore.

  Mushrooms, walls, the fence and your own tail are deadly. Hearts drift
  across the field: catch one for 100 points and a bigger bonus
  multiplier (up to x9). The bonus counts down from 1000; what is left
  at the end of the round, times the multiplier, is added to your score.

  STUCK? Boxed yourself in? Press R or hold fire for a second to retry
  the round. It costs a life; the round starts again as it was. Losing a
  life also restarts the round.

  12 rounds; after round 12 they come back with a faster bonus.
  Extra life every 10000 points.

SCORES
  Clover 20   Ball 50   Heart 100   Bonus left x multiplier

CONTROLS
  Joystick in port 2, or the cursor keys: one step per push
  Fire / Space / Return   start
  R  or hold fire         retry the round (costs a life)
  P                       pause
  M                       music on / off
  Esc                     back to the title (on the title: quit)

NEEDS
  Amiga 500, 68000, Kickstart 3.1 / Workbench 3.1, 1 MB chip RAM
  (it also runs with 512K). Runs on an ACA500(plus) and in WinUAE.

ON A HARD DISK / CF CARD
  Double-click Install on the game disk, or unpack the hard drive zip,
  or use the WHDLoad version (F10 quits WHDLoad).
