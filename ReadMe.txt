An Amiga 500 arcade puzzle game, inspired by the 1986 MSX game Snake It.

THE GAME
  Steer your snake across the meadow and eat every clover. Each clover
  makes the snake longer - and makes a white ball pop up somewhere on
  the field. The balls must be eaten too, but they don't make you grow.
  A round is clear when every clover and every ball is gone.

  Mushrooms are poison. Walls, the fence and your own tail are deadly.
  Now and then a heart drifts across the field: catch it for 100 points
  and a bigger bonus multiplier (up to x9).

  The bonus counts down from 1000. What is left at the end of the round
  is added to your score, times the multiplier. When the bonus runs out,
  new mushrooms start to sprout - so don't dawdle!

  12 rounds; after round 12 they start again, faster.
  Extra life every 10000 points.

SCORES
  Clover 20   Ball 50   Heart 100   Bonus left x multiplier

CONTROLS
  Joystick in port 2, or the cursor keys
  Fire / Space / Return   start
  P                       pause
  M                       music on / off
  Esc                     back to the title (on the title: quit)

NEEDS
  Amiga 500, 68000, Kickstart 3.1 / Workbench 3.1, 1 MB chip RAM
  (it also runs with 512K). Runs on an ACA500(plus) and in WinUAE.

ON A HARD DISK / CF CARD
  Double-click Install on the game disk, or unpack the hard drive zip,
  or use the WHDLoad version (F10 quits WHDLoad).
